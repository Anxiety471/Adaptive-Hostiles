# Changelog

## Unreleased

- A decision-model key is required: with no key (or the provider set to Off) the mod stays out and raids are vanilla.
- No elite Operative pawn is spawned any more.
- Ranged raiders spread around a colonist in the open from several angles, even when they can see it.
- Every raid is commanded by the squad from its first tick; no Operative is needed.
- Ranged raiders kite by weapon range, hold at outranging distance, and keep the kite/all-in choice for 30 seconds.
- Melee attack colonists in the open instead of staging for the base; gathering ends when colonists stand in the open.
- Ranged raiders with nobody in sight reposition from several angles.
- Melee keep attacking turrets until they fall and step out of blast range.
- One-opening buildings: a small group goes through, the rest hold their own spots.
- Raiders no longer leave on their own while commanded; the squad lets go when no colonist is left.
- Squad decision every two seconds; optional image reading of the colony.
